MenuHelp — Installation

Requires macOS 15.7 or later. Supports Apple Silicon and Intel.
Official download: https://github.com/mrbonifacio/MenuHelp/releases/latest

1. Open the DMG and drag MenuHelp into Applications.
   For ZIP downloads, extract and move MenuHelp.app to Applications.
2. Open MenuHelp from Applications. Its icon appears in the menu bar.
3. For the free, non-notarized build, macOS may block the first launch.
   After attempting to open it, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security >
   Open Anyway and confirm, if you trust the download source.
   You do not need to disable Gatekeeper or run Terminal commands.
4. Allow MenuHelp in Privacy & Security > Accessibility when requested for
   automatic pasting or keyboard cleaning.

No Homebrew, Xcode, m1ddc, ddcctl or Rosetta installation is required.
Brightness helpers are bundled. Hardware brightness requires a DDC/CI-capable
external monitor with DDC/CI enabled and a compatible cable, port or dock.
The MacBook's built-in display is not controlled by these helpers.

Clipboard history shortcut: Shift + Command + V.
Language and launch at login: Settings in the menu bar.
To update, quit MenuHelp and replace the app in Applications. Your preferences
and history remain on this Mac. Accessibility may need to be granted again.
Do not run the app directly inside the DMG.

Bundled licenses and full helper sources are inside the app's Resources folder
(Show Package Contents), under Licenses and ThirdPartySources.zip.
Apple instructions: https://support.apple.com/102445
